Metrics a network does not measure are left out rather than reported as zero. Telegram, Bluesky and Slack have no impressions or reach at all — their engagement is measured against followers, and on Slack the only metric there is at all is reactions — so an absent key means 'not measured here', never 'nobody saw it'.
